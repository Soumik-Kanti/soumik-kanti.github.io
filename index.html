About Me

I am a passionate Software Quality Assurance Engineer with 6+ years of experience across iOS, Android, Web, API, and OS-level testing. My professional journey includes working on mobile applications, core banking systems, customized Android operating systems, and IoT products, which has given me strong cross-domain QA expertise and a deep understanding of software quality standards.

Currently, I lead QA activities for 30+ live iOS applications at Appnap Technologies Ltd, including health, communication, and photo-editing products such as Device Tracker, Blood Pressure Monitor, Heart Rate Monitor, and Dazz Cam. I am responsible for test planning, black-box testing, API validation, automation testing using Playwright, Selenium, and Appium, along with ensuring smooth SDLC and STLC implementation throughout the release cycle.

Previously, I worked at LEADS Corporation Limited, where I contributed to testing core banking solutions including BankUltimus, XChecker, and FSIBL Cloud. My responsibilities included functional testing, database validation, UAT support, and real-world beta testing in multiple banking environments, providing me with valuable exposure to enterprise-level business workflows and customer-focused quality assurance.

Earlier, I started my career as an R&D Engineer at OPPO and realme, where I developed a strong QA foundation through extensive OS-level and device testing. My experience included network testing, field testing, compatibility testing, camera and sensor validation, performance testing, stability testing, and IoT device quality assurance for large-scale mobile products.

I am known for strong ownership, analytical thinking, problem-solving ability, and effective collaboration with developers, designers, product managers, and business teams. I enjoy learning new technologies, improving QA processes, and contributing to reliable, scalable, and high-quality software solutions.
